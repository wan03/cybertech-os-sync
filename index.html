Cybertech OS Sync

Internal file synchronization between Google Drive and the Cybertech server.

About

Cybertech OS Sync is a private operational tool used by authorized Cybertech staff to synchronize business files between Google Drive and the Cybertech server. It supports document intake, report distribution, and recovery.

The application is not a consumer product and is not offered to the general public. It is used internally by Cybertech personnel who have been granted access.

Google Drive access

The application uses Google Drive authorization to read, download, upload, update, and synchronize files. These operations are what make two-way synchronization between Drive and the Cybertech server possible.

Although the OAuth permission granted by Google allows broad access to a user's Drive, the application's configured sync targets the Cybertech folder. Access is requested at the level Google's permission model provides; use is scoped by the application's configuration.
