Privacy Policy

Cybertech OS Sync · Effective September 15, 2026

This policy describes how Cybertech OS Sync handles data when an authorized user connects their Google Account to the application.

Data we access

The application accesses file contents and metadata in the configured Cybertech Google Drive folder. This includes business documents and reports, along with file metadata such as names, identifiers, folder locations, timestamps, and sizes.

Google's Drive permission granted to the application allows broad access to the user's Drive. The application's configured sync targets the Cybertech folder.

Why we access it

Access is used for synchronization between Google Drive and the Cybertech server, document intake, report distribution, and recovery. The application reads, downloads, uploads, updates, and synchronizes files to carry out these functions.

Where copies are stored

Copies of synchronized files are stored on the Cybertech server and in Google Drive. Local recovery backups are also maintained.

Who can access it

The application and the data it synchronizes are intended for authorized Cybertech personnel.

Credentials and tokens

OAuth credentials and access tokens are stored privately on the Cybertech server, outside the synchronized project directory. They are not included in the files the application synchronizes.

Revoking access

You can revoke the application's access to your Google Account at any time through your Google Account's third-party connections settings, at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Revocation stops any future authorized access by the application. It does not automatically delete copies or backups that already exist on the Cybertech server or in Google Drive. To request deletion of existing copies, contact us using the address below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
